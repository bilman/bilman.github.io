Film/TV Work

I was a Technical Advisor: Mathematics Consultant for the TV series Citadel (Amazon) produced by AGBO and Amazon Studios, starring Priyanka Chopra Jones, Richard Madden, Roland Møller, and Stanley Tucci. My role involved:

  • Reviewing and consulting on the mathematical content in the scripts

  • Creating all mathematical elements for the show, including on-screen writing and background material

  • Collaborating with the production team during the preproduction and filming stages.
