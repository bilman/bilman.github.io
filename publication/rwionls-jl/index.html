Abstract

Julia package for computing the general rogue waves of infinite order for the focusing nonlinear Schrödinger equation. This is a family of special solutions of the focusing nonlinear equation denoted by $\Psi(X,T;\mathbf{G}, B)$, whose exact and asymptotic properties (in different regimes where $(X, T) \in \mathbb{R}^2$ becomes large) were studied recently by D. Bilman and Peter D. Miller and were found to exhibit anomalous dispersion.
